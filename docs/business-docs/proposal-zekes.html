Zeke's Comedy Club — Market Context & Positioning SitesByMac  ·  April 2026  ·  Supplemental Research

Know Your Market

  • 55–60 seat intimate basement room in a festival & lake-season corridor
  • Racing weekends, Lakefest, and IC Fest drive strong seasonal foot traffic
  • Beer-forward, whiskey-friendly crowd; high group-table turnover
  • Nearest clubs (Dayton, Columbus, Cincinnati): all urban-booked and hours away
  • Zeke’s can own the authentic Midwest room identity by default
  • Invisible to anyone outside Celina without a web presence

Bar Strategy & Hospitality

  • Pitchers and 100oz beer towers: high margin, fast to pour, group-friendly
  • Simple whiskey cocktails (Old Fashioned, Jack & Coke) fit the demo perfectly
  • Intermission bar boost adds $200–$300 per seating
  • Uber/Lyft promo codes on receipts reduce liability and build goodwill
  • Designated driver raffle: free soft drinks + 2 free tickets to a future show
  • DD program builds goodwill with local police, city, and festival organizers

Demographics & Spending Power

  • Median household income: $62,849–$66,142; solidly middle-class market
  • 60%+ of households earn $50k–$200k: the entertainment-spending sweet spot
  • 27% earn $100k–$200k; the group-outing, high-spend segment
  • Cost of living index 94: 6% below national average; disposable income stretches further
  • Unemployment 1.9%; median rent $814/mo; financial stability is high
  • $66k in Celina buys the lifestyle of $75k–$80k in Columbus or Dayton

Stay Close, Spend Local

  • Most residents commute under 20 minutes; city traffic is actively avoided
  • Trips to Dayton or Columbus happen once a month or less
  • Celina is the regional hub for dining, shopping, and lakefront activity
  • St. Henry, Coldwater, and Rockford all drive in for their nights out
  • A visible, consistent venue becomes the default weekend plan
  • Festival & lake-weekend visitors are already here looking for nighttime options

Midwest Touring Pipeline

  • Over 500+ working comics within a 3-hour drive: Chicago, Detroit, Cleveland, Columbus, Cincinnati, Indianapolis
  • Features and openers actively seek paid routing stops between major club weekends
  • A tape-friendly room with warm crowds is a rare and marketable asset
  • 55–60 seats is ideal for rising comics landing their first headlining weekend
  • Word spreads fast in regional scenes; one good room becomes a repeat stop
  • Celina’s low cost (gas + one night lodging) makes it an easy yes for routing comics

Cross-Promotional Opportunities

  • Lakefest & IC Fest: sponsorship table, print promo, social cross-tagging
  • Eldora Speedway: comedy show bundled into race-weekend packages
  • Mercer County Fair: on-site promo booth and ticket giveaways
  • Local restaurants: pre-show dinner deal combos drive early arrivals
  • Hotels & B&Bs: “comedy weekend” package rates for out-of-town guests
  • Celina Tourism, Lake St. Marys visitor bureau, WCSM radio, and the Chamber of Commerce
