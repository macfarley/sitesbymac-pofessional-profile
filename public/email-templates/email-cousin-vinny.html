A Personal Word From the Family

Dear Valued Guest,

It has come to my personal attention that you submitted a topping request through our suggestion box. First of all — we read every one of those. Every single one. That's how seriously we take this business.

Your suggestion was kangaroo. 🦘

Now, I want you to know that I sat down with my cousin Sal, my Aunt Teresa, and our head of kitchen operations — my other cousin Sal — and we gave this the full consideration it deserved. We had a conversation. A long one. Over espresso.

After careful deliberation, we have decided that kangaroo will not be joining the Cousin Vinny's menu at this time. Or any time. This is not personal. This is business. And the business is pizza. Dayton pizza. The kind your grandmother would recognize.

We do, however, appreciate the enthusiasm. As a token of our respect, please accept a coupon for a free order of garlic knots on your next visit. The kangaroo situation is behind us. We say no more about it.

The family thanks you for your loyalty. Come hungry.

 

With respect and marinara,
